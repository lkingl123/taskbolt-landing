# TaskBolt Distribution Playbook

**Created:** May 11, 2026
**Premise:** Local Utah only, scope = small-batch jobs that fit in a car, no shipping.
**Strategy:** Cast wide. Anywhere a Utah human might see it and fit the scope, that's a channel.
**Realistic week 1 commit:** ~9 hours. Expect first inquiry in 7-14 days.

---

## TIER 1: This week (highest ROI, lowest effort)

| # | Channel | Action | Time | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Local Utah FB groups** | Post in 5-8 groups (list below) | 1 hr | Free |
| 2 | **Nextdoor** | Post in home zip + 5-10 neighbor zips | 30 min | Free |

## Realistic tracking

After week 1, track:
- # of posts/places posted (count)
- # of inbound DMs / emails / calls (response rate)
- # of conversations that turned into a quote (qualification rate)
- # of quotes that became jobs (close rate)

A normal funnel for a brand-new local service:
- 50 posts/visits → 5-15 conversations → 2-5 quotes → 1-2 first jobs

Don't beat yourself up if it takes 4-6 weeks. That's the standard.
