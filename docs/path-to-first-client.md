# Path to First Client — TaskBolt Local Labor

**Created:** May 11, 2026
**Goal:** First paying client by end of June 2026
**Premise:** LLC filed (pending approval), website live at taskbolt.work, positioning locked in.

---

## Critical Path (the 5 things that *must* happen)

1. LLC approves → unblocks bank account + legal protection
2. Bank account opens → unblocks accepting payment
3. Cards + workspace ready → unblocks looking professional in person
4. Target list + first outreach → unblocks inbound conversations
5. First quote → first client → unblocks proof of execution

Everything else is helpful but not blocking.

## Phase 2: Make It Legal & Bankable (right after LLC approves)

**Timeframe: 1-2 days after LLC approval arrives**

- [ ] Open Mercury business bank account at mercury.com — needs EIN letter + Articles of Organization + ID. ~20 min, free
- [ ] Cancel the old DBA at businessregistration.utah.gov → Manage Entities → find "Taskbolt" → Cancel DBA. ~10 min
- [ ] Connect Mercury to Stripe (already have Stripe from the hub) — collect payments before bank fully active
- [ ] Set up Stripe Invoicing — free, sends professional invoices via email
- [ ] Update website footer: `© 2026 Taskbolt LLC` instead of `© 2026 TaskBolt`
- [ ] (Optional) Draft a simple single-member LLC Operating Agreement — some banks ask for it eventually

## Phase 7: Convert the First Client (week 5-6)

**Timeframe: when first real inquiry comes in**

- [ ] Respond within 2 hours to any inquiry — speed is your biggest edge
- [ ] Ask 4 questions before quoting:
  - What's the work? (service)
  - How many units? (volume)
  - When do you need it? (deadline)
  - What's your address? (pickup feasible?)
- [ ] Quote per cheat sheet within 4 hours of inquiry
- [ ] Send Stripe invoice for 50% deposit before pickup
- [ ] Do the work to your highest standard — first client = reputation
- [ ] Take before/during/after photos (with permission)
- [ ] Send a thank-you note + final invoice when done
- [ ] Ask for a written testimonial + permission to use their name as a reference
- [ ] Ask for one referral: "Who else do you know who could use this?"
